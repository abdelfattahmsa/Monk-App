## Identity

| | |
|---|---|
| **App name** | Monk |
| **Tagline** | Your Rule |
| **Positioning** | Personal Mastery OS |
| **Company** | Kyberia Labs |
| **Stage** | Alpha (→ Beta Aug 2026) |
| **Suite** | Monk · Nomad · Mizan |

**What Monk is:**
Not a productivity app. A serious tool for people who treat their life like a business — tracking and optimising the four resources that determine everything: Money, Time, Energy, Health.

**What Monk is not:**
A habit tracker. A todo list. A journaling app. A religious companion (that's Mizan).

---

## The Four Pillars

```
💰 Money      ⏱ Time       ⚡ Energy     ❤️ Health
```

Every feature in Monk maps to one of these pillars. Goals orchestrate across all four.

## Logo Usage

The Monk wordmark uses the "M" mark on a `#22C55E` square (12px border radius at 48px size). The mark should never appear on a background lighter than `#1A1A1A` in dark contexts.

**Logo sub-line:** `PERSONAL MASTERY OS` — 9–11px, 2.5px letter-spacing, uppercase, `#888888`.

**Reserved space:** Minimum 16px clearance on all sides of the logo mark.

## What Not to Do

1. **Do not add religious content to Monk.** Deen, prayer, Quran — those belong in Mizan.
2. **Do not use the gold color for primary actions.** Gold is for badges and milestones only.
3. **Do not soften the brand.** Monk is not friendly-productivity; it is serious mastery.
4. **Do not use the "PRP" name anywhere.** The rebrand is complete.
5. **Do not ship features that don't map to a pillar.** Every screen belongs to Money, Time, Energy, or Health — or to Goals/Overview which orchestrate across them.
